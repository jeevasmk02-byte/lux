CHAPTER FIVE · 05

DEAR LUX ♡

IF WORDS COULD
HOLD A HUG...

En life-la nee evlo important-nu words-la explain panradhu romba kashtam, Lakshmi. 🥺 Indha 7 years journey-la namma evlo memories create pannirukkom, evlo sandhoshama sirichirukkom, evlo silly things pannirukkom, evlo fights pottirukkom, but edhu nadandhaalum namma bond mattum enakku eppovume special. ♾️❤️

Nee enakku just oru friend mattum illa, nee en caretaker, en happiness, en support system, enakku edhu venumnaalum first nyabagam varra person. 🫂💞 Enna care panradhu, enna understand panradhu, enakku pidichadha nyabagam vechikiradhu, naan silent-ah irundhaalum enna purinjikiradhu nu nee enakkaga pannina neraya vishayangal irukku. 🥹💕

Sila neram naan unakku evlo important-nu solla marandhirukkalam, but honestly Lux, nee en life-la irukkuradhu enakku oru periya blessing. 🙏❤️ Enakku one and only female best friend-nu sonna adhu nee mattum dhaan. 🥺🌍 Unna maadhiri oruthanga life-la kidaikkuradhu romba rare.

Indha 7 years-la nee enakku pannina ovvoru chinna chinna vishayamum en manasula special-ah irukku. 🥹💗 Namma friendship eppovum ippadiye irukkanum, innum neraya memories create pannanum, innum neraya years ippadiye sanda pottu, sirichu, tease panni, orutharukku oruthar support-ah irukkanum. 🫂♾️

Life-la unakku evlo kashtangal vandhaalum adhellam overcome panni nee nenachadhellam achieve pannanum. ✨ Un face-la irukkura smile eppovum disappear aaga koodadhu. 😊🌸 Nee deserve panra ella happiness-um unakku kidaikkanum. 💖 Oru vishayam mattum eppovum marandhudadha, unakku eppovume oru special place en life-la irukkum. ❤️

Years maaralam, situations maaralam, namma life busy aagalam, aana indha 7 years bond and memories enakku eppovume priceless. 🥺♾️ Thank you for being my caretaker, my happiness and one of the most important persons in my life. 🫶💗 Unna friend-ah kidaichadhukku naan genuinely lucky, Lux. 🥹❤️
